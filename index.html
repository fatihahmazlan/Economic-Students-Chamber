About ESC

A community for curious economists

ESC is a student-led society for economics students in Brunei. We make economics practical, data-driven and social.

01

Our vision

To be Brunei’s leading student community for economic thinking — where curiosity, evidence and public-spirited ideas meet.

02

Our mission

To connect economics students across Brunei with data, skills, mentors and each other, through research, events and tools that make economics accessible.

03

Our objectives

  • Build analytical and data skills
  • Encourage research on Brunei’s economy and Wawasan Brunei 2035
  • Bridge students with industry, government and academia
  • Make economic data easy to find and understand
  • Grow a supportive, inclusive community

Regression lab

Run a regression in your browser

Simple OLS with standard errors, p-values and a chart. Paste your own data or pull two World Bank indicators for the same country. Everything runs locally on your device.

Tip: use plain numbers (no thousands separators). A header row is ignored.

Years are matched between the two series. Time-series regressions can show spurious relationships — treat results as a starting point, not proof.
